The concept

What is a flop?

Every great invention hides behind it a long series of failures. Failed prototypes, abandoned ideas, projects mocked by contemporaries — and yet essential to progress.

The « Flop or Not? » exhibition invites you to flip the perspective: what if what we call failure is actually the necessary condition for all success? Through unusual objects, unpublished archives and interactive installations, the museum invites visitors to reconsider their relationship with error.

Programme

The programme from March 12 to May 30, 2027

Over two months to explore, question and vote. Here is an overview of the 2027 edition programme at the Musée des Arts et Métiers.

From 1:00 PM — Opening

Welcome & free visit

Doors open. Free exploration of the permanent exhibition and spaces dedicated to the flops of history.

🚪 Free entry

Mondays — Talk

"The history of great mistakes"

A historian of science traces the inventions that were first flops before becoming indispensable.

🎤 Talk

Wednesdays — Workshop

Make your flop!

Group creative workshop: imagine and build the most useless invention possible. The public votes for the ultimate flop.

🛠️ Participatory workshop

Saturdays — Debate

Flop or not? The big vote

Presentation of the 10 most controversial inventions in the museum. The public votes live: flop or misunderstood genius?

🗳️ Public vote

Sundays — Round table

Meet the inventors

Contemporary creators share their own failures and what they taught them.

💬 Round table

May 30 — Closing

Awarding of the Flop Prize 2027

The public names the most failed invention of the year. Festive ceremony and closing cocktail.

🏆 Ceremony

Venue

Musée des Arts et Métiers

Founded in 1794, the Musée des Arts et Métiers holds exceptional scientific and technical collections. Affiliated with the Cnam, it houses over 80,000 objects and is the ideal setting for questioning innovation and its detours.
